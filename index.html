Personal WHOOP integration

Privacy Policy

My WHOOP Life ChatGPT — a personal, non-commercial integration to retrieve and analyze the app owner's WHOOP information.

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Personal-use application. This application is intended solely for its developer's own WHOOP account. It is not offered as a public service to other WHOOP members.

1. Who operates this application

My WHOOP Life ChatGPT (the “App”) is a personal project operated by an individual developer for the developer's own use. It uses the official WHOOP API with the account owner's authorization.

2. Information accessed

Depending on the permissions authorized, the App may retrieve WHOOP recovery information (such as recovery score, HRV and resting heart rate), physiological cycles and strain, sleep records, workouts, basic account profile details, and body measurements.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are used for authentication. The App does not request the account owner's WHOOP password.

3. Purpose of processing

The App uses this information only to present personal health and fitness trends, review recovery, sleep and activity, and respond to analysis requests initiated by the account owner. Results are informational and do not constitute medical advice or diagnosis.

4. Storage, disclosure and third-party services

  • OAuth tokens are intended to be stored on the owner's local device, not in this public GitHub repository.
  • The App is not intended to maintain a separate permanent database of WHOOP health records. Data retrieved for a query may be handled in memory or in locally generated files as needed for that query.
  • When the owner chooses to analyze WHOOP data with ChatGPT, the selected information is sent to OpenAI to process the request, subject to OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy policy and account settings.
  • If Postman is used during initial OAuth authorization, its callback infrastructure may handle the authorization response. Postman is not the intended destination for ongoing health-data analysis.
  • WHOOP information is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally made publicly accessible.

OpenAI, WHOOP and (if used) Postman have their own policies governing their respective services. This policy covers the developer's personal integration, not those third-party platforms.

5. Retention and security

Local tokens and any optionally saved local files remain under the device owner's control and may be deleted by the owner. ChatGPT conversations and data submitted to OpenAI are governed by OpenAI's own retention settings and policies. The owner should not upload credentials to GitHub or share them in chats.

6. Choices and revocation

The owner can discontinue use, delete locally stored tokens or files, and revoke the App's access in the WHOOP connected-app authorization settings. The owner can also manage information submitted to ChatGPT using the available ChatGPT privacy controls.

7. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the App's data handling changes. Material changes should be documented here before a new workflow is deployed.

8. Contact

For questions about this personal project, please use the project's GitHub Issues page. Do not include health information, API tokens or other sensitive details in a public issue.
